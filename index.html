    How to Use GonaBot Bauchi

    Your complete guide to every feature of your AI farm assistant

    Chat
    • Ask any farming question in English or Hausa
    • Tap the quick suggestion chips for common questions
    • Each reply shows a language badge (EN/HA) and a Save + Copy button
    Daily Advisor
    • Select your LGA from the dropdown to load today's live weather for your area
    • Pick a crop — Groundnut, Maize, or Tomato — to get advice tailored to that crop
    • The AI generates 4 plain-language bullet points telling you exactly what to do on your farm today based on current temperature, humidity, rain chance, and wind
    Weather
    • Select your Bauchi LGA and tap "Get Today's Advice"
    • Real-time weather loads with a skeleton preview while fetching
    • AI farming advice is generated based on today's conditions
    Pest Alert
    • Select affected crop, tick symptoms, then tap "Diagnose Now"
    • Results appear as expandable flashcards with a severity badge
    • Each card has a Hausa translation toggle, Copy, and Save button
    • Share diagnosis directly to WhatsApp for other farmers
    Advisor
    • Fill in your farm profile — LGA, budget, soil, season and more
    • Get a personalised crop advice broken into 4 flashcard sections
    • Toggle Hausa translation per card, Share to WhatsApp, or Save
    Saved Responses
    • Tap "Save" on any AI reply to bookmark it locally on your device
    • Access saved items by tapping the menu icon (☰) in the top right corner and choosing "Saved Responses"
    • Works offline — no internet needed to read your saved advice
    Saved Responses
    • Tap Save on any bot reply, pest result, or advisor output to bookmark it
    • Open your saved items anytime from the menu icon (☰) in the top right — reads fully offline, no internet needed
    • Share any saved advice directly to WhatsApp in one tap
    Tips for Best Results
    • Be specific — mention your LGA, farm size, or crop variety in the Chat tab
    • Use the Daily Advisor tab for weather-based crop tips every morning
    • Toggle light mode with the moon/sun button in the header for outdoor use
    • Access your saved responses anytime from the menu icon (☰) in the top right
